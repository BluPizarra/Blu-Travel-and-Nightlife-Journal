## The 10 Curated Destinations
### 5 Island Escapes (Daylight Zen)
1. **Bohol** — Loboc river cruising, Chocolate Hills, and rural countryside exploration.
2. **Boracay** — Powder-white flour sand, neon tangerine sunsets, and beachfront celebrations.
3. **Balabac** — Untamed southwestern Palawan frontier, virgin sandbars, and off-grid tranquility.
4. **Baguio** — Pine-scented mountain breeze, vintage thrifting, indie art spaces, and Session Road fog.
5. **Marinduque** — Heartbeat-of-the-culture local magic, unhurried coastal drives, and serene traditions.

### 5 Nightlife Hotspots (Midnight Electric)
6. **AKAI Katipunan, QC** — Student strip ground zero, raw college-town spontaneity, and non-stop sing-alongs.
7. **McKinley Whisky Park, Taguig** — Open-air BGC skyline, ambient jazz, and sophisticated pours into the AM.
8. **QU Poblacion, Makati** — Erratic neon pulse, intimate strangers turned comrades, and peak YOLO memories.
9. **URBN Poblacion, Makati** — Heavy-bass arena, flashing strobe lights, and synchronized dancefloor energy.
10. **Reverie Poblacion, Makati** — Sultry mood lighting, hypnotic exhale, and intimate late-night conversations.

## Technical Highlights
* **CSS Grid & Flexbox:** Mobile-first architecture that seamlessly transitions from multi-column desktop views into single-column layouts on mobile screens (`@media (max-width: 768px)`).
* **Interactive Cartographic Map:** Custom SVG map of the Philippines and Metro Manila Inset with interactive pins, dynamic island/nightlife filtering, and a non-obtrusive destination preview panel.
* **Base64 Optimization:** High-resolution destination photographs embedded as Base64 data URIs for instant, reliable rendering on any device or network.
* **Full-Resolution Lightbox:** Interactive modal allowing visitors to inspect 2048px photography in full fidelity with zero cropping.
* **Expandable Read More Teasers:** Articles page starts with punchy brief summaries and expands into full 2-paragraph reflections upon clicking "Read Description".
